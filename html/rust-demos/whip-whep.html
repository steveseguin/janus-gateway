Local (WHIP publisher — canvas capture)

Remote (WHEP subscriber)

This demo publishes a canvas-captured bouncing ball + 440 Hz sine tone via WHIP (HTTP POST of SDP offer to /whip), then subscribes to the published stream via WHEP (HTTP POST to /whep/{publisher_id}). No janus.js required — pure standard WebRTC signaling over HTTP.
You can also open the standalone WHEP player in a separate tab and paste the Publisher ID to watch from a different client.
